As your business scales, so does your financial complexity. An audit is an independent examination of your financial information to ensure accuracy and compliance with accounting standards and tax laws.

Types of Audits We Handle

  • Statutory Audit: Mandatory under the Companies Act for all Private Limited and Public Limited companies, regardless of turnover.
  • Tax Audit (Section 44AB): Required if your business turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore (₹10 Crore for primarily digital transactions) or gross professional receipts exceed ₹50 Lakhs.
  • GST Audit: While the mandatory GSTR-9C threshold is ₹5 Crore, voluntary audits help prevent costly departmental notices for all businesses.

Strategic Tax Planning

Compliance is just the baseline. Strategic tax planning involves structuring your business transactions, investments, and capital expenditures to legally minimize your tax liability — before the financial year ends, not after.
