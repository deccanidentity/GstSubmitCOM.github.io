Maximize Your Tax Savings

Strategic tax planning can help you keep more of your hard-earned money. Only eligible taxpayers can claim these deductions in their income tax returns. Such eligible taxpayers have been specified under various sections of the Act.

Note: Taxpayers who opt for the New Tax Regime can only claim deductions under sections 80CCD(2) and 80JJAA. Deductions should be reduced from the gross income to reach the taxable amount.

Section Deep Dives

Section 80C: The Gold Standard

This is the most popular tax-saving section. You can claim up to ₹ 1,50,000 by investing in:

ELSS Mutual Funds
Public Provident Fund (PPF)
Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
Home Loan Principal
Life Insurance Premium
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

Section 80D: Health & Security

Medical insurance is crucial. Section 80D allows deductions for premiums paid:

  • Self, Spouse & Children: ₹ 25,000
  • Parents (Below 60): ₹ 25,000
  • Parents (Senior Citizens): ₹ 50,000
  • Preventive Health Checkup: ₹ 5,000 (within limits)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim deduction under both Section 80C and Section 80CCC?
Yes, you can claim a deduction under both, subject to the overall combined limit of ₹ 1.5 Lakh.
Is investment in NPS over and above 80C?
Yes, Section 80CCD(1B) provides an additional benefit of up to ₹ 50,000 over and above the ₹ 1.5 Lakh limit of 80C.
Are SIPs eligible for tax deduction?
Only SIPs in Equity Linked Saving Schemes (ELSS) qualify for tax deductions under Section 80C. Other mutual fund SIPs are not eligible.
Can I claim 80D for my father-in-law?
No, Section 80D can only be claimed for self, spouse, dependent children, and parents. Father-in-law is not included.
