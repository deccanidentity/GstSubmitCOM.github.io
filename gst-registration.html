GST Registration Turnover Thresholds (2024)

GST registration is mandatory once your aggregate annual turnover exceeds the limits specified below in a financial year. Keep in mind that aggregate turnover is calculated on a PAN-wide basis across all locations in India.

Business Type Normal Category States Special Category States
Suppliers of Goods ₹40 Lakh ₹20 Lakh
Service Providers ₹20 Lakh ₹10 Lakh

Mandatory Registration (Regardless of Turnover)

There are specific scenarios where obtaining a GSTIN is legally required from day one, regardless of your annual revenue. These include:

  • Businesses making inter-state supplies of goods.
  • Casual taxable persons (e.g., selling goods at temporary exhibitions).
  • Non-resident taxable persons.
  • Entities selling goods through e-commerce platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, or Swiggy.
  • Businesses required to pay tax under the reverse charge mechanism (RCM).

Key Compliance Updates for 2024/2025

To curb tax evasion and ensure seamless matching of input tax credits, the GST ecosystem has introduced stricter verification measures:

1. Zero Mismatch Policy

The GST portal now enforces a policy where discrepancies between your GSTR-2B (input tax reported by suppliers) and your GSTR-3B (claims made by you) can lead to the immediate blocking of return filing until the error is rectified.

2. Stricter Verification & Aadhaar Biometrics

New registrations are subject to increased scrutiny. Physical verification of business premises is now extremely common for high-risk sectors. Additionally, Aadhaar-based biometric authentication is increasingly being rolled out to verify applicant identity.

3. Bank Account Mandate

Failure to furnish valid bank account details in your GST profile shortly after registration can lead to the automatic suspension of your GSTIN.
