Privacy Policy

At GSTSubmit, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our tax compliance services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you request a callback, sign up for our services, or upload documents for tax filing. This may include:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, and PAN.
  • Financial Information: Bank statements, invoices, and previous tax filings.
  • Business Information: GSTIN, company registration details, and financial records.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Process and file your GST and Income Tax returns.
  • Communicate with you regarding your filings or service updates.
  • Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Data Security

We implement bank-grade security measures to protect your data. This includes 256-bit AES encryption for data at rest and TLS for data in transit. Your documents are stored on secure cloud servers with restricted access.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Government Portals: Necessary for filing your returns.
  • Certified Professionals: Our partner CAs who review and file your taxes.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting and communication tools that help us operate.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information stored with us. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@gstsubmit.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
