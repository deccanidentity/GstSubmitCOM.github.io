Step-by-Step ITR e-Filing Process

  1. Login / Create Account: Sign in using your registered mobile number, email ID, or PAN details to start the filing process.
  2. Enter Personal Information: Provide basic details such as PAN Number, Aadhaar Number, Date of Birth, Address & Contact Details, and Bank Account Information.
  3. Select Income Sources: Choose your applicable income categories: Salary Income, Business or Professional Income, Freelance Income, House Property Income, Capital Gains, or Other Sources of Income.
  4. Upload or Enter Financial Details: Add your income and tax details manually or through documents like Form 16, Salary Slips, Bank Statements, TDS Certificates, and Investment Proofs.
  5. Claim Deductions & Exemptions: Declare eligible deductions under applicable sections such as Section 80C, Section 80D, Home Loan Benefits, Education Loan Interest, and Donations & Other Tax Savings.
  6. Tax Calculation: The system calculates Total Income, Tax Liability, Refund Amount (if applicable), and Outstanding Tax Payable.
  7. Review & Verify Details: Carefully review all entered information before submission to avoid errors or mismatches.
  8. Submit Income Tax Return: File the ITR online securely through the e-filing process.
  9. Complete E-Verification: Verify the filed return using Aadhaar OTP, Net Banking, Bank Account Verification, Demat Account Verification, or EVC (Electronic Verification Code).
  10. Download Acknowledgment: After successful verification, download the ITR acknowledgment for future reference.
