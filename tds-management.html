Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is a mechanism where tax is deducted at the point of origin of income. As an employer or business making payments (rent, professional fees, commissions), you are required to deduct TDS and remit it to the government.

TDS Compliance Cycle

Managing TDS involves strict adherence to deadlines to avoid interest and late fees (₹200 per day under Section 234E).

  • TAN Registration: Obtaining a Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number — required before any deduction.
  • Monthly Payments: Depositing the deducted tax by the 7th of the following month via Challan 281.
  • Quarterly Returns: Filing Form 24Q (salaries), 26Q (non-salaries), or 27Q (NRI payments) by the due date.
  • Certificate Issuance: Generating and issuing Form 16 or 16A to all deductees.
