Complete Guide for Small Business & Freelancers

Learn how TAXSubmit simplifies your compliance journey.

What is TAXSubmit?

TAXSubmit is an Indian tax and compliance platform that helps individuals and businesses file Income Tax Returns (ITR), GST Returns, TDS Returns, and Business compliance reports. It is especially useful for proprietors, freelancers, consultants, and small business owners.

Auto-Import Form 26AS & AIS

Form 26AS: Your tax passbook containing TDS deducted by clients, advance tax paid, and self-assessment tax.

AIS (Annual Information Statement): Includes bank interest, mutual funds, stock transactions, and GST turnover.

How we use this: We fetch your TDS entries and bank income automatically, reducing manual entry and preventing missing income that could trigger notices.

GST + ITR Integration

The Income Tax Department compares GST turnover, bank transactions, and ITR sales turnover. Mismatches can trigger notices. TAXSubmit helps by importing GST sales, matching invoices, and reconciling turnover differences.

Profit & Loss Support

Business owners filing ITR-3 need a P&L Statement and Balance Sheet. We provide structured forms where you can enter sales, purchases, rent, salary, and other expenses. Our software automatically calculates taxable business income.

ITR-3 vs ITR-4 (Sugam)

ITR-3: For business owners maintaining proper books of accounts (Shop owners, Traders, etc.).

ITR-4: Best for freelancers and small businesses under Presumptive Taxation (Section 44AD/ADA). You don't need detailed books in many cases.

Step-by-Step Filing Process

  1. Create Account: Sign up with PAN and Email.
  2. Import Data: Connect PAN to fetch AIS/26AS.
  3. Select Type: Choose Freelancer or Business Owner.
  4. Enter Details: Add turnover and expenses.
  5. Verify: Check tax payable or refund amount.
  6. File & E-Verify: Submit via Aadhaar OTP or Net Banking.
